CONSTRAINT CHECK (load-bearing — do not skip):
The OPERATOR CONSTRAINTS block above is the operator's own words, not a paraphrase of
them. Before you report DONE, add a `CONSTRAINT CHECK` section to your report with one
row per constraint id, and one of exactly four verdicts:

- `PASS` — you exercised the behaviour this constraint governs and it holds. Cite the
  check you ran. A row whose `scope` is `user-visible` may read `PASS` only when it is
  backed by a REAL-PRODUCT observation: a screenshot, a rendered page, a CLI transcript
  of the actual surface. Unit and integration evidence alone grades `NOT-EXERCISED`.
- `VIOLATED` — the work contradicts it. **This is a BLOCKING item regardless of what any
  upstream artifact says.** An upstream artifact that authorised the violation is a
  *second* finding: name it, with its file and section.
- `NOT-EXERCISED` — you did not test the behaviour this constraint governs. State why.
- `NOT-APPLICABLE` — this constraint's `scope` gives your stage nothing to confirm. Say
  which scope and why your stage cannot reach it.

If a constraint's `Binds` scope is contradicted by a mechanism its `Asked about` scope
does not name, that is `VIOLATED`, not an out-of-scope observation. `Binds` is expected to
be WIDER than `Asked about`; a `Binds` that merely repeats its `Asked about` is itself a
finding — report it.

If the ledger is empty, quote its `none_recorded_reason` in your CONSTRAINT CHECK section
and say whether you believe it. Silence is not a pass.

**If your stage APPLIES a plan rather than judging one** (`coding`, `coding-fix`,
`fix-code`, `qa-fix`): a constraint that contradicts your plan is **not** licence to
deviate. Stop, record `VIOLATED`, and escalate with `forge ask`. Editing off-plan to
satisfy a constraint is the failure the SCOPE DIFF CHECK exists to catch. **Your rows may
read `VIOLATED` or `NOT-APPLICABLE` only, never `PASS`** — a mechanical executor asserting
that a constraint holds is self-certification, which the bridge's evidence rule (a
worker's `--status DONE` is a CLAIM, never evidence) exists to prevent.

`NOT-EXERCISED` counts toward your `BLOCKING_ITEMS` count **only for `scope: user-visible`
rows**. On `scope: internal` rows the honest declaration belongs on your `NOT_COVERED:`
line instead of halting the pipeline.

Report every id in the ledger, including ones you graded `NOT-APPLICABLE`. An id you omit
is indistinguishable from an id you forgot, and the bridge checks set equality in both
directions.
